When two mods that are on change the same game file, only one of them shows in game. The mod
higher in the load order wins. Change the load order when the wrong mod shows, for example when
two skins for the same champion are on.

Each profile has its own load order.

## Prerequisites

- The patcher is stopped.
- **Sort by** is **Priority**. See [Find a mod](/manager/library/find-mods/#sort-the-library).
- No search, no filter and no selected mods.

## Move a mod

1. Drag the mod's card up or down.

   A line shows where the mod will go.

2. Release the card.

The next time you start the patcher, the new order applies.

## How folders affect the order

You can move a mod only inside its own group: inside one folder, or among the mods that are not in
a folder. To move a mod to another group, see
[Organize mods into folders](/manager/library/folders/).

Mods that are not in a folder win over mods inside folders, even though the grid shows folders
first.

## Move mods to the top when you turn them on

To make the mod you turn on win over the others, turn on **Move enabled mods to front** in
**Settings** > **Library**. With this setting on, a mod you turn on moves to the top of its folder.
