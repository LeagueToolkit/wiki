Select several mods to act on all of them at once. When you select a mod, the selection bar shows
at the bottom of the page.

Selecting a mod does not turn it on or off. A plain select on a card turns the mod on or off.

## Select mods

| To                             | Do this                                                                             |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Add one mod, or remove it      | Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and select the card, or select its checkbox                    |
| Add every mod between two mods | Select the first mod's checkbox, then hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> and select the last mod |
| Select every mod you can see   | Select **Select all**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd>                        |
| Clear the selection            | Select **Select all** again, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>                                |

The checkbox shows in the top-left corner of a card when you point at it, and on every card while
mods are selected.

**Select all** selects only the mods the grid shows. In a folder, it selects the mods of that
folder. With a search or a filter, it selects the mods that match.

## Act on the selection

In the selection bar, select one of these:

- **Enable _n_** or **Disable _n_**: turn the selected mods on or off. The mods stay selected.
- **Check health _n_**: check the selected mods for problems. See
  [Check and repair mods](/manager/library/mod-health/).
- **Uninstall _n_**: delete the selected mods. See [Uninstall mods](/manager/library/uninstall-mods/).

**Enable**, **Disable** and **Uninstall** are not available while the patcher runs.

## Limitations

- You cannot drag mods to change the load order while mods are selected.
- The selection clears when you leave the Mods page.
- You cannot move the selected mods into a folder at once. Move each mod separately.
