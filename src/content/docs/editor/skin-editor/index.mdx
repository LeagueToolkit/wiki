The skin editor is the class view of a skin object in the [bin editor](/editor/bin-editor/). It
draws the skin's mesh on its skeleton, plays the clips of its animation graph, and shows the
skin's fields beside the character they build.

## Open a skin

A skin is the object at `Characters/<Character>/Skins/Skin<N>`, of class
`SkinCharacterDataProperties` or `TftSkinCharacterDataProperties`. Open it from the
[Objects browser](/editor/objects-browser/), or expand it in its bin and select **Open object**.

The object tab opens in the **Skin** view. Switch between **Skin** and **Properties** with the view
mode control in the tab header.

## Panes

A skin tab holds five panes. Drag a pane's tab to move it, and drag the edge between two panes to
resize them. The **Panes** menu turns a pane on or off, and **Reset layout** puts every pane back.

| Pane      | What it shows                                                               |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Preview   | The character, posed by a clip, with the preview controls and the transport |
| Clips     | The clips of the skin's animation graph, and its tracks, masks and groups   |
| Spells    | The character's spells, and ability sequences to preview on the character   |
| Material  | One material the skin draws with, as tables you can edit                    |
| Inspector | The skin's fields, grouped in sections                                      |

By default, **Preview** fills the left side with **Clips** and **Spells** under it, and
**Material** sits over **Inspector** on the right.

## Preview

The preview draws the skin's <Hint as="span" tip="Skinned mesh file format. Contains vertex data, bone weights, and material references for character models." client:visible>[SKN](/glossary/)</Hint>
mesh on its <Hint as="span" tip="Skeleton file format. Defines the joint hierarchy and bind pose for character models." client:visible>[SKL](/glossary/)</Hint>
skeleton, wearing its idle effects. The camera frames the character when the skin loads.

<Screenshot
  file="editor/skin-editor-preview-controls.webp"
  alt="The preview controls in the top right corner of the skin preview"
/>

| #   | Name                | Description                                                                   |
| --- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Map backdrop        | Stands the skin on a map. See [Map backdrop](#map-backdrop)                   |
| 2   | Move                | Shows a gizmo that moves the character. Select it again to turn the character |
| 3   | Ground              | Draws a ground plane under the character. Shows while no backdrop is on       |
| 4   | Midlane             | Gives the ground the texture of a Summoner's Rift lane                        |
| 5   | Effects             | Shows the idle effects and the particle events of the playing clip            |
| 6   | Open effect         | Opens an idle effect or a particle event of the clip in its own tab           |
| 7   | Armature            | Draws the skeleton over the character                                         |
| 8   | Hexshade            | Draws the skin with the game's own shaders. See [Hexshade](/editor/hexshade/) |
| 9   | Bake tangents       | Saves tangents into the mesh. See [Bake tangents](#bake-tangents)             |
| 10  | Submeshes           | Shows or hides each submesh of the mesh                                       |
| 11  | View mode           | Draws the scene lit, unshaded, untextured or as a wireframe                   |
| 12  | Camera              | Moves the camera to a preset view                                             |
| 13  | Frame the character | Moves the camera back to the whole character                                  |
| 14  | Hide viewport UI    | Hides the controls and the transport. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to show them       |

**Effects** shows only when the skin has idle effects or the clip has particle events. **Bake
tangents** shows only when the skin's bin is in a layer of your project.

**Move** has a menu, **Move options**. Choose **Move** or **Turn** for the gizmo, or select **Back
to the centre** to put the character back where it started.

**View mode** draws the scene **Lit**, **Unshaded**, **Untextured** or as a **Wireframe**.
**Wireframe overlay** draws the wireframe over the other modes. **Anti-aliasing** is **Off**,
**FXAA (in game)** or **SMAA**.

**Camera** moves the camera to the **Game**, **Orbit**, **Top**, **Front** or **Side** view.

### Transport

The transport under the preview plays, pauses and scrubs the clip, and sets the playback speed.
Its picker chooses the clip that poses the skin. **Bind pose** shows the skeleton's rest pose.

A clip that blends between values shows a **Parameter** slider. The preview plays the part of the
clip whose value is nearest the slider.

| Key                                                 | Action                             |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| <kbd>Space</kbd>                                    | Play or pause                      |
| <kbd>Left</kbd> / <kbd>Right</kbd>                  | Pause and step one frame           |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Left</kbd> / <kbd>Right</kbd> | Pause and step a tenth of a second |
| <kbd>Home</kbd>                                     | Restart the clip                   |
| <kbd>F</kbd>                                        | Frame the character                |
| <kbd>[</kbd> / <kbd>]</kbd>                         | Slower or faster, from 0.05x to 2x |

The keys work while the focus is in the preview. Select the preview to give it the focus.

### Map backdrop

**Map backdrop** stands the skin on a map of your installation. Its menu, **Map backdrop
options**, lists **None** and each map. Each map opens a list of its map skins, and you choose one
of them. The menu also has four switches:

<dl>
  <dt id="backdrop-particles">Particles</dt>
  <dd>Plays the particle systems placed on the map.</dd>

<dt id="backdrop-event-effects">Event effects</dt>
<dd>Adds the effects a map event shows. Works only while **Particles** is on.</dd>

<dt id="backdrop-structures">Structures and props</dt>
<dd>Draws the map's structures and props.</dd>

  <dt id="backdrop-sky">Sky</dt>
  <dd>Draws the map's sky.</dd>
</dl>

With a backdrop on, three more controls show. **Visibility layers** turns each layer of the map on
or off. **Sun** and **Post effects** start from the map's own values, which you can change for the
preview. Each has a reset back to the map's values. None of these controls change your files.

- **Sun** sets **Sun color**, **Sun strength**, **Azimuth**, **Elevation**, **Sky color**,
  **Ground color** and **Ambient strength**. **Reset to the map's sun** puts them back.
- **Post effects** holds **Depth fog**, **Height fog**, **Depth of field** and **Ambient
  occlusion**, each with its own settings. **Reset to the map's post effects** puts them back.

### Armature and submeshes

**Armature** draws a dot for each joint and a line to its parent. Its menu holds **Names**, which
writes each joint's name beside its dot.

**Submeshes** lists the submeshes of the `.skn` file. Turn one off to hide it, or on to show it,
whatever the skin and the playing clip say. **Show as the skin does** drops your choices.

### Bake tangents

**Bake tangents** saves mesh tangents into the `.skn` file, for physically based materials with
normal maps and roughness-metallic textures. The mesh must be in the same layer of your project as
the skin's bin. When it is not, the button is disabled and its tooltip says so.

## Clips pane

The **Clips** pane lists the skin's animation graph as a table. Select a row to pose the preview
with that clip. The transport's picker then names the clip, and a play mark shows on its row.

<Screenshot
  file="editor/skin-editor-clips.webp"
  alt="The Clips pane with the Clips, Tracks, Masks and Sync groups tabs, a filter and the clip table"
/>

- The **Clips**, **Tracks**, **Masks** and **Sync groups** tabs switch between the four maps of
  the graph.
- **Filter** narrows the rows by name.
- The caret at the start of a row unfolds the clip's own fields. <kbd>Right</kbd> and
  <kbd>Left</kbd> unfold and fold the focused row.
- Select a track, mask or sync group chip to switch to its tab, with the entry marked.
- Right-click a row and select **Open animation**, or **Open animation to the side**, to open its <Hint as="span" tip="Animation file format. Stores per-joint keyframe data for champion and map animations." client:visible>[ANM](/glossary/)</Hint>
  file.

On the **Masks** tab, select a mask to see where it reaches on the character. The parts the mask
does not weigh dim, and the armature turns on. Select the mask again to let it go.

The **Clips** tab has these columns:

| Column     | What it shows                                                        |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name       | The clip's name, and its kind                                        |
| Rate       | The frame rate of the animation file, then the clip's tick rate      |
| Track      | The track the clip plays on                                          |
| Mask       | The mask the clip plays through. Shows when a clip names one         |
| Sync group | The sync group the clip keeps time with. Shows when a clip names one |
| Events     | The number of events in the clip                                     |

See [Animation graph](/editor/animation-graph/) for the graph itself.

## Spells pane

The **Spells** pane stages a visual cast on the character, called an ability sequence. It lists
the sequences saved in your project under **Ability sequences**, and the character's spells from
your League installation under them. Enter text in **Find a spell** to narrow the spells.

To start a sequence, select **Create ability**, or select a spell to start one from that spell's
data. The pane then shows the sequence's fields:

1. Choose the **Animation**, the **Cast bone** and the effects.
2. Set the timing and the target.
3. Select **Preview sequence** to play it in the **Preview** pane.
4. Select **Save recipe** to keep it in your project.

A saved recipe shows only for its character. **Remove recipe** deletes it. A spell that the preview
cannot play shows **Not supported yet**, **Unavailable** or **Conflicting definitions** in place of
its play mark.

<dl>
  <dt id="ability-animation">Animation</dt>
  <dd>The clip the character plays. **No animation** keeps the character in its bind pose.</dd>

<dt id="ability-cast-bone">Cast bone</dt>
<dd>The joint the cast starts from. **Character origin** uses the character's position.</dd>

<dt id="ability-cast-effect">Cast effect</dt>
<dd>The effect that plays on the cast bone from the start of the sequence until the release.</dd>

<dt id="ability-release">Release at (s)</dt>
<dd>The second of the sequence at which the cast is released.</dd>

<dt id="ability-projectile">Projectile effect</dt>
<dd>The effect that flies from the cast bone to the target.</dd>

<dt id="ability-missile-delay">Missile delay (s)</dt>
<dd>Seconds between the release and the projectile leaving the cast bone.</dd>

<dt id="ability-flight">Flight duration (s)</dt>
<dd>The seconds the projectile takes to reach the target.</dd>

<dt id="ability-impact-effect">Impact effect</dt>
<dd>The effect that plays at the target when the projectile arrives.</dd>

<dt id="ability-impact-time">Emit for (s)</dt>
<dd>The seconds the impact effect plays.</dd>

<dt id="ability-target">Target X, Target Y, Target Z</dt>
<dd>The point in the preview where the cast lands.</dd>

  <dt id="ability-guides">Range guides</dt>
  <dd>
    Outlines of the spell's range and radii, as suggested at rank one. They are visual guides, not
    collision shapes.
  </dd>
</dl>

A sequence's timings must finish within 30 seconds.

## Material pane

The **Material** pane shows one material that the skin draws with, as the
[Material editor](/editor/material-editor/) draws it. Select a submesh on the character to show its
material, or pick another in the pane's **Material** list.

The character shows a material edit only with Hexshade on. While it is off, the pane offers **Draw
with Hexshade**.

## Inspector

The **Inspector** shows the skin's fields in sections. The
[Skin editor reference](/editor/skin-editor/reference/) describes each field.

| Section            | What it holds                                                   |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identity           | The skin's name for sound events, its classification and parent |
| Icons              | The portraits and the loading-screen art                        |
| Mesh               | The mesh, the skeleton, the textures and the material           |
| Material overrides | A row per submesh that draws with its own material or textures  |
| Animation          | The animation graph                                             |
| VFX                | The idle effects, each with the system its key resolves to      |
| Audio              | The sound banks                                                 |
| Health bar         | The health bar settings                                         |
| Other              | Every field that no other section holds                         |

Hover over a material override to dim every submesh but the one it dresses. Select a part of the
character to scroll its override into view.

## Limitations

- A clip that the game picks at run time, from a condition or a chance, plays its first child that
  has an animation file.
- An ability sequence is a visual cast that you time by hand. It does not play the animation's
  events, and its range guides are not collision shapes.
