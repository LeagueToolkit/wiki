Hexshade is the shading engine of LTK Editor. It takes the shaders from your League installation
and runs them in the editor, so a 3D viewport draws League content the same way the game does. Its
purpose is an accurate picture of how your content will look in game, without starting a game.

<Video
  file="editor/hexshade-skin"
  alt="A skin in the skin preview, first with the editor's standard shading, then with Hexshade on"
/>

## Why it matters

Without Hexshade, a viewport draws with a general-purpose material that only comes close to
League's look. A texture or a material setting can then look right in the editor and wrong in game,
and you find out only when you test.

With Hexshade, the viewport runs the shader that the material names in the game data, with the
material's textures and parameters. What you see is what the game draws.

## How it works

Hexshade is the editor's registry and manager for every shader:

- **It reads the game's shader bundles.** The shaders come from your League installation, so they
  are always the ones your game uses, also after a patch.
- **It translates them while you work.** Hexshade turns a game shader into one that WebGL can run
  the first time a viewport needs it, not in advance. A viewport that shows new content can take a
  moment to draw it with Hexshade.
- **It shares them.** Every viewport gets its shaders from the same registry, so a shader is
  translated once and then reused.

## Turn on Hexshade

Hexshade works in every 3D viewport that shows League content. It is off by default.

1. Open a viewport, for example the skin preview or the map preview.
2. In the viewport toolbar, select **Hexshade**, the button with the swirl icon.

   The viewport draws again with League's shaders. In the map preview, this includes the map's
   lightmaps and its sun.

## Edit materials live

In the character skin preview, you can edit a material and see the change at once, drawn with the
game's shader. Select a submesh on the character to open its material next to the preview. See
[Material editor](/editor/material-editor/).

Live material editing works only in the skin preview for now. The map preview will get it later.

## View modes

The view mode menu in the viewport toolbar changes what the viewport draws. It shows the current
mode, for example **Lit**. The choice applies to every viewport, and LTK Editor keeps it.

<dl>
  <dt id="lit">Lit</dt>
  <dd>Full shading with lights. With **Hexshade** on, this is the view that matches the game.</dd>
  <dt id="unshaded">Unshaded</dt>
  <dd>Textures without lighting.</dd>
  <dt id="untextured">Untextured</dt>
  <dd>Lighting on plain surfaces, without textures.</dd>
  <dt id="wireframe">Wireframe</dt>
  <dd>Only the edges of the meshes.</dd>
  <dt id="wireframe-overlay">Wireframe overlay</dt>
  <dd>The edges drawn over the current view mode.</dd>
</dl>
